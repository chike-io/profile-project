Current Courses

  • CSC331 - Programming Principles and Paradigms

    Introduction to different programming principles, paradigms, and techniques for solving programming problems.

  • CSC334 - Systems Programming

    Study of system-level programming, operating system concepts, and interaction between software and computer systems.

  • CSC301 - Computer centre management

    Covers the management, operation, maintenance, and administration of computer centres and their resources.

  • CSC313 - Computer architecture and Organization

    Covers the structure and operation of computer systems, including processors, memory, input/output systems, and how hardware components work together.

  • CSC321 - Operating systems

    Covers the management of computer resources, processes, memory, files, and other functions that allow software and hardware to work together.

  • CSC322 - digital communication systems

    Covers the transmission, processing, and exchange of digital information through communication systems and networks.

  • CSC341 - Data structures

    Covers the organization, storage, and manipulation of data using structures such as arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, and graphs.
